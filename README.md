## Role and company research

The current DRW Research Engineer (Options) role is based in Chicago and works as part of a systematic trading team with technologists, quantitative researchers, and traders. The team studies and simulates strategies, builds trading algorithms and ergonomic tools, improves the research lifecycle, and takes suitable ideas from prototype to production. The role combines production Python, quantitative finance, Linux/concurrent/low-latency systems, customer collaboration, and optional low-level language experience.

DRW describes its business as proprietary trading across global markets, using its own capital and combining technology, market research, and risk management. The portfolio reflects the research-engineering work loop: desk problem → model and tools → realistic simulation → production feedback.
